SecureLend, Inc. is a US (Delaware) company. These pages cover the lender underwriting MCP for business clients, and a separate consumer marketplace.

## Lender underwriting

The lender server is `https://mcp.securelend.ai/v1/lender/mcp`. Its tool list covers resolving a listed company, reading a filing, opening a case, screening sanctions, drafting a credit memo, and opening a hosted package. No tool approves a loan. A hosted package page states that no credit decision is recorded.

- [Lender MCP](/mcp/lender)
- [Start a 7-day trial](https://securelend.ai/login?trial=1)
- [Talk to us](https://securelend.ai/contact)

There is no public price.

## Consumer marketplace

`https://mcp.securelend.ai/mcp` compares consumer and small-business financing. It is not the underwriting server.
